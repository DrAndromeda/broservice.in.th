🔧 What a Typical Visit Includes

The work usually centres on the services listed above: oil, brake pads, chain, tires and engine diagnostics. A visit typically starts with the symptom you describe, then a look at the basics — oil level, brake feel, chain condition, tire wear and lights — before the agreed job is done.

  • Checking what you reported and confirming the cause where it can be seen on the spot
  • Doing the agreed repair, on-site or in the shop depending on the job
  • Pointing out anything else that looks worn, so you can decide whether to deal with it now or later

For renters, the basic maintenance check covers oil, brakes, tires and lights.

🏍️ Who Needs It and When

Island roads, steep hills and salt air wear bikes faster than most people expect. This service is useful for:

  • Tourists and short-term renters whose scooter is stalling, hard to start or making a new noise
  • Long-term residents and renters who want regular checks instead of waiting for a breakdown
  • Riders whose brakes squeal or feel soft after the hills around Haad Rin
  • Anyone stuck far from a workshop, for example on a backroad near Chaloklum, who needs to know if it is a roadside fix

📝 How to Prepare Before You Call

A little information makes the phone diagnosis faster and helps bring the right parts:

  • Note what happens and when: only when cold, only under load, after rain, or all the time
  • Record a short video if there is a noise, or take a photo of the damaged part or tire
  • Know the bike's type and size (scooter or manual) and roughly how old it is
  • Send your location by pin on WhatsApp or Telegram, and park the bike somewhere flat and safe
  • If the bike is rented, it helps to check with the owner before major parts are replaced
  • Keep the key handy and take your belongings off the bike

💵 What Affects the Final Price

The table above shows ranges because the exact cost depends on the job. Typically the main factors are:

  • Which service is needed, and whether it is charged per wheel or per tire
  • The brand and quality of the parts you choose
  • The type and size of the bike, and how worn the parts already are
  • Whether the work can be done on-site or needs the shop
  • Extra faults found during the work, which are discussed before anything more is done

As in the booking steps above, you get a clear price before work starts.

🧰 When Something More Is Needed

Not every problem is a quick roadside job. The phone diagnosis is there to sort this out early. Typically the bike will need the shop, or a longer visit, when:

  • The engine has a deeper fault than a quick adjustment or service can solve
  • The bike has been in a fall or crash and the frame, forks or wheels may be damaged
  • Several systems are failing at once, such as brakes, lights and starting together
  • The bike is unsafe to ride — in that case it is better not to ride it to us, but to call and arrange a visit

If a part has to be ordered or the repair is outside what can be done at your location, you will be told before work begins.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Do you come to my location?

Yes, mobile service is available across the island for most common repairs.

How often should I get maintenance done?

Every couple of months for regularly-used scooters, given local terrain and coastal air.

Can you fix a bike that won't start?

In most cases yes — we diagnose on the phone first to bring the right parts.

How do I contact BroService?

Call +66 80-870-5704, WhatsApp, Telegram or email. Available 8:00–20:00 daily.

What should I tell you when I call about a bike problem?

Describe the symptom and when it happens, the type of bike (scooter or manual), and your location. A short video of any noise helps a lot with the phone diagnosis.

Is it safe to keep riding with squeaking or soft brakes?

Squealing can be a worn pad, but soft or weak braking is a safety issue. It is best to stop riding and have them checked, especially before steep hills.

Can I get a rented bike repaired?

It is usually possible, but it helps to check with the rental owner first, especially if parts need to be replaced.
