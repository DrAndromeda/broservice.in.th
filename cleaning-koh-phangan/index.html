🧽 What a Visit Typically Includes

A standard visit usually focuses on the areas that get used every day. The exact scope is worth confirming when you describe your space.

  • Kitchen surfaces, sink and hob
  • Bathrooms, including toilet, shower and basin
  • Floors, dusting and wiping of reachable surfaces
  • Linen change, if fresh linen is available
  • Deep clean add-ons on request: windows, inside of appliances, post-construction dust

🏝️ Who Needs It on Koh Phangan

Cleaning tends to be most useful for people whose homes are not occupied or are in constant turnover.

  • Owners of holiday villas who need the property ready between guests
  • Long-term renters who want regular upkeep without organising it themselves
  • Guesthouse and condo hosts preparing for new arrivals
  • Tenants and owners handling a move-in or move-out
  • Anyone finishing a renovation, when fine dust settles on everything
  • Owners who are away for part of the year and want the place fresh on return

🔑 How to Prepare Before the Visit

A little preparation helps the clean go faster and avoids misunderstandings.

  • Put away valuables, documents and fragile items
  • Arrange access in advance: be present, or agree how the team will enter
  • Tell us about pets, allergies or surfaces that need gentle treatment
  • Point out priorities, such as a stained floor or a very dirty oven
  • Mention parking or access issues for hard-to-reach villas
  • Send a few photos if the condition of the property is unusual

💵 What Affects the Final Price

The prices above are a guide. The final amount typically depends on:

  • The size of the property and number of rooms and bathrooms
  • The type of clean: regular, deep, move-out or post-construction
  • The current condition, for example heavy grease, mould spots or thick dust
  • Extras such as windows, inside of appliances or a large terrace
  • Whether the clean is one-time or part of a repeating schedule

🔍 When a Different Specialist Is Needed

Cleaning can remove the symptoms of some problems but not the cause. It helps to call a specialist first if you notice:

  • Recurring damp or mould that returns after cleaning, which may point to a leak or ventilation problem and calls for a plumber or handyman
  • A weak or bad-smelling air conditioner, which needs an AC technician rather than a surface clean
  • Broken appliances or fittings that need repair, not cleaning
  • Large amounts of building debris or a property that is still under construction: describe the situation first so the right type of clean can be agreed
