🔌 What an Electrical Visit Typically Includes

A visit usually starts with a look at the problem itself and at the breaker box, followed by testing to find where the fault actually is. Depending on the job, that can mean tracing which circuit is affected, checking sockets, switches and connections, replacing a faulty part, or installing a new fitting, then testing before the power goes back on.

🏝️ Who Needs an Electrician and Common Situations on Koh Phangan

  • Long-stay residents — a dead socket, a flickering light or a breaker that trips whenever the kettle or air conditioner runs.
  • Villa owners and rental managers — checks before guests arrive, before the rains, or after a property has stood empty for a while.
  • New tenants and buyers — a safety inspection before moving in, since wiring in older bungalows can be hard to judge by eye.
  • Anyone renovating — new lighting, fans or a water heater often need the wiring behind them to be checked first.

🧰 How to Prepare Before the Visit

  • If it is safe to reach, switch off the affected circuit at the breaker box. If you smell burning, see scorch marks or the area is wet, stay away, switch off at the main only if you can do so without standing in water, and call us.
  • Do not touch wet sockets or exposed wires, and do not keep resetting a breaker that trips again straight away.
  • Note what happens and when: which rooms are affected, what was running, and whether the problem appears after rain.
  • Send a photo or short video of the problem and of the breaker box if you can. It helps us understand the job before we come.
  • Make sure someone can give access to the property and the breaker box, and clear the area around it.
  • Plan for parts of the property to be without power for a while, for example by saving work and moving food out of the fridge if needed.

🏷️ What Affects the Final Price

The price table above gives the typical ranges. 🔍 When a Site Visit or Another Specialist Is Needed

Some problems cannot be priced or solved from a message. Repeated tripping, burning smells, damaged wiring, or a large rewiring job usually need someone to look at the installation on site first. Larger jobs often require a site visit before a quote can be firm.

If the electrics turn out to be fine and the fault lies in the appliance itself, such as an air conditioner or water pump, a specialist for that equipment may be the better next step. If water is leaking near wiring, the leak needs fixing by a plumber as well as the electrical repair. For a bigger renovation, it is worth planning the electrical work together with the other trades.
