Handyman on Koh Phangan — Home Repairs & Maintenance

✅ Handyman on Koh Phangan — BroService fixes furniture, mounts TVs, repairs walls, paints, and handles general home maintenance across the island. One call fixes it all. Call +66 80-870-5704 or WhatsApp.

Not every job needs a specialist. Sometimes you just need someone reliable to show up and fix the small stuff — a wobbly shelf, a door that won't close, furniture that arrived in twelve boxes. BroService.in.th covers the everyday repairs that don't fit neatly into one trade.

Our handyman service handles furniture assembly, TV and shelf mounting, curtain rails, door and window adjustments, minor wall repairs, and general troubleshooting around the villa. If a job turns out to be something bigger — a wiring fault behind a wall, or a plumbing issue under a sink — we'll say so honestly and bring in our electrician or plumber rather than patch over it.

Most small jobs get a flat-rate quote over the phone once you describe what's needed. Bigger visits with several tasks are quoted on-site, and it's common to bundle multiple small jobs into a single call-out — just list everything when you book so we bring the right tools the first time.

🧰 What a Handyman Visit Typically Includes

A visit usually centres on the jobs you listed when you called. Typically that means putting furniture together, hanging a TV, shelf or curtain rail, easing a sticking door or window, tightening loose hinges and fittings, and patching small holes or cracks before touch-up paint. Because the work is general rather than one trade, several unrelated small fixes can often be done in one go.

🏝️ Who Needs a Handyman on Koh Phangan

Common situations include:

  • Owners and managers of rental villas who need small faults fixed between guests
  • Newcomers who have bought flat-pack furniture and want it assembled properly
  • Long-stay residents whose doors, windows and shelves have shifted or loosened over time
  • People who are away part of the year and come back to a list of minor repairs
  • Anyone with a mix of small jobs that does not justify calling several different trades

Humid, salty island air is hard on hinges, locks and painted surfaces, so these small repairs tend to come up regularly.

📝 How to Prepare Before the Visit

  • Write down every job, even the tiny ones, and give the full list when you book
  • Say where the work is: which room, and whether it is indoors or outdoors
  • For mounting, mention the wall type if you know it (brick, concrete, wood or lightweight board) and the size or weight of the TV or shelf
  • Have the item, its brackets and its fittings ready; keep flat-pack boxes and instructions together
  • Photos or a short video sent by WhatsApp or Telegram help a lot with the quote
  • Clear the work area and make sure someone can give access to the property

🔎 What Affects the Final Price

  • How many items or tasks there are, and whether they are bundled into a single visit
  • The size and weight of what is being mounted or assembled
  • The wall or surface material, and how hard the spot is to reach
  • Whether the job needs extra materials such as fixings, filler or paint, and who supplies them
  • The condition of the item: a damaged door frame takes longer than a simple adjustment
  • Travel distance to the location and how much time the work takes

Small single jobs described clearly over the phone are usually quoted flat; multi-task visits are quoted on-site.

🧑‍🔧 When a Specialist or a Site Visit Is Needed

Some problems look small but are not handyman work. A fault that may involve wiring, a leak or damp coming from inside a wall, or a problem with pipes under a sink should be seen by an electrician or plumber, and we will say so honestly. A site visit makes sense when there are many tasks, when the cause of a problem is unclear, or when a wall or structure looks damaged and the right fix cannot be judged from a description or photos. In those cases it is better to look first and quote afterwards.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

What counts as a handyman job versus a specialist job?

General repairs, assembly, mounting and installation. If it turns out to involve wiring or plumbing behind a wall, we bring in the right specialist rather than patch over it.

Can I bundle several small jobs into one visit?

Yes, that's common — just list everything when you call so we bring the right tools and materials the first time.

Do you charge a flat rate?

Small single jobs are usually flat-rate once described over the phone; multi-task or larger visits are quoted on-site.

How do I contact BroService?

Call +66 80-870-5704, WhatsApp, Telegram or email. Available 8:00–20:00 daily.

Who supplies the materials, such as fixings, filler or paint?

Mention it when you call. Whether you already have the parts or need them for the job affects the quote, so it helps to agree who supplies what before the visit.

Can a TV be mounted on any kind of wall?

It depends on the wall material, the weight and size of the TV and the bracket. Brick, concrete, wood and lightweight board are all treated differently, so describe the wall and send a photo of the spot and the TV if you can.

What if my flat-pack furniture has missing or damaged parts?

Check the contents against the instructions before the visit. Assembly can only be done with what is in the box, and missing or damaged parts usually need to be sorted out with the seller first.
