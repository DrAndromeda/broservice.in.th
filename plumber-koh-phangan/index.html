🔍 What a Plumbing Visit Typically Includes

A visit typically starts with locating the source of the problem, then repairing or replacing the faulty part, and finally checking that water flows, drains and shuts off properly. For a dripping faucet that may mean a new cartridge or washer; for a clog, clearing the drain and testing that it runs freely; for a pipe, cutting out the damaged section and joining new pipe in its place. Where a fixture or heater cannot be saved, we can talk through replacement options with you.

🏝️ Who Needs a Plumber on Koh Phangan

  • Villa and house owners — dealing with leaks, low pressure or slow drains, especially in older houses where salt air and hard water have worn fittings.
  • Rental managers — turning properties around between guests, when a running toilet or dripping tap needs fixing quickly.
  • Long-stay residents and renters — who want to report a problem to the landlord with a clear idea of what is wrong.
  • Owners of new builds — for fixture installation, water heater setup or a check before guests or tenants move in.

🧰 How to Prepare Before the Visit

  • Note where the problem is, when it started and whether it is constant or only happens when a certain tap or appliance is used.
  • Send a short video or a few photos by WhatsApp or Telegram — it often helps with the phone diagnosis.
  • If water is escaping, close the nearest isolation valve or the main supply, if you know where it is.
  • Clear the area under sinks and around the toilet or water heater so the plumber can reach the pipes.
  • Make sure someone can give access to the property, and tell us if the water heater model or its age is known.

📊 What Affects the Final Price

The price depends on the type of job and several practical factors, which is why a clear estimate is given before work starts:

  • how easy the pipe or fixture is to reach, for example behind a tiled wall or under a slab;
  • the condition and material of the existing pipes and fittings;
  • the number of fixtures or drains involved;
  • the parts needed, and whether a part can be repaired or must be replaced;
  • the distance to your location on the island and the urgency of the call-out.

⚠️ When You Need a Different Specialist or a Site Visit

Most leaks, clogs and fixture problems can be handled by a plumber, but some situations need a closer look. If the cause is not clear from photos and a phone description, an on-site visit is usually the only reliable way to quote correctly. Persistent damp patches, a repeatedly blocked drain or low pressure throughout the whole house may point to a hidden pipe fault or a problem with the water supply itself. If the issue involves wiring around a water heater or pump, an electrician should check it; if water has already damaged walls, ceilings or tiles, a handyman or builder may be needed for the repair afterwards.
