🧳 Who Needs It and Common Situations on Koh Phangan

People usually contact us in one of these situations:

  • Newcomers arriving on the island who do not yet know which area suits them
  • Remote workers and digital nomads who need a stable place with reliable internet for a few months
  • Long-stay visitors who want to move out of a hotel or guesthouse
  • Families and couples who need more space, a garden or parking
  • Owners and residents changing homes who want a second pair of eyes at a viewing

📝 How to Prepare: What to Send Us

The more specific your request, the easier it is to suggest suitable options. When you write to us on WhatsApp or Telegram, it helps to include:

  • Your monthly budget range
  • Preferred area, or what matters most (beach, shops, quiet, yoga, nightlife)
  • Move-in date and planned length of stay
  • Number of people, including children
  • Pets, if any
  • Whether you will use a scooter or a car, and if you need parking
  • Internet needs, for example if you work online
  • Must-haves such as a pool, air conditioning, a kitchen or a workspace

💰 What Affects the Final Rent

The table above shows typical ranges. Where a property lands within a range, or outside it, usually depends on:

  • Location and distance to the beach, shops and the main road
  • Property type, size and number of bedrooms
  • Sea view, pool and garden
  • Furnishing and the condition of the building
  • Length of the contract, since longer stays often allow more flexibility
  • Season and demand at the time you search
  • Whether electricity, water and internet are included or billed separately

🔍 What to Check Before Signing Any Rental

This is general advice for any rental, not legal advice, and it applies whoever helps you find the place:

  • See the property in person, in daylight, and check the neighbourhood at different times of day
  • Get the agreement in writing and read it fully, including the term, the rent, and the notice period
  • Make an inventory and take dated photos of the condition, furniture and appliances at move-in
  • Clarify the deposit: the amount, what it covers, and when and how it is returned
  • Ask how electricity and water are charged and who pays for repairs
  • Test the water pressure, hot water, air conditioning and the internet signal
  • Check the access road, especially how it looks after heavy rain
  • Keep receipts for every payment

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

How early should I start looking for a rental?

It depends on the season and what you need. Many people start a few weeks before arrival, and some prefer to arrive and view properties in person first. Send us your move-in date and we can discuss realistic options.

Can I rent a place if I have pets or a scooter?

Rules differ from owner to owner. Mention pets and parking at the start, so unsuitable options are filtered out before you spend time on viewings.

Should I pay a deposit before seeing the property?

It is generally safer to view the property first, read the written agreement, and pay only against a receipt that states what the payment is for.
