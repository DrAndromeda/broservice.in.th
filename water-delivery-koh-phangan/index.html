Water Delivery on Koh Phangan

✅ Water Delivery on Koh Phangan — BroService delivers 19L bottled water to your villa or condo, same-day in most areas. Scheduled delivery, pump & cooler rental. Call +66 80-870-5704 or WhatsApp.

Running out of drinking water is one of the most common calls we get on the island — and one of the fastest to fix. BroService.in.th delivers 19L bottled water directly to your villa, condo or guesthouse anywhere on Koh Phangan.

Most orders in the Thong Sala and Srithanu area go out the same day; further-out addresses near Chaloklum or Baan Tai are usually next-day. For long-term renters we set up scheduled recurring delivery so you never run dry — weekly or bi-weekly, adjusted to your household size.

We also rent out pumps and coolers if your current setup is broken or you're moving into a new place. Payment is simple: cash on delivery, bank transfer, or PromptPay. Regular customers on a recurring schedule get a small discount on standing orders.

📍 Areas We Cover

Our water delivery service covers all Koh Phangan: Thong Sala, Srithanu, Haad Rin, Chaloklum, Baan Tai, and surrounding areas.

🧾 What Water Delivery Typically Includes

A standard order is 19L bottles brought to your door, with the quantity and address agreed by phone or message. Around that, the service typically covers:

  • one-off orders when you are running low, or a recurring weekly or bi-weekly schedule
  • a manual pump or a hot & cold cooler on rent if your place has no working setup
  • larger quantities for guesthouses, offices and events
  • payment by cash, bank transfer or PromptPay

🏝️ Who Needs It: Common Use Cases on Koh Phangan

Many residents and visitors prefer bottled water for drinking, so 19L bottles are an everyday item in rented homes across the island. Typical situations:

  • long-term renters in villas and condos who want a steady supply without carrying heavy bottles
  • new tenants moving in who need a pump or cooler from the first day
  • guesthouse and small-business owners whose guest numbers change from week to week
  • families, groups and retreats staying a few weeks, or events that need a one-time larger quantity
  • property owners preparing a house before guests arrive

📝 How to Prepare Before You Order

A few minutes of preparation makes the first delivery smoother:

  • count the people in the household and estimate how many bottles you use per week; start with that number and adjust after the first deliveries
  • check whether you already have a pump or cooler and whether it works, and say so if you need a rental
  • send your address with a map pin or a short description of how to find the house, especially on smaller side roads
  • decide how you will pay: cash, bank transfer or PromptPay
  • tell us where bottles can be left or who will be there to receive them
  • if you need same-day delivery in Thong Sala or Srithanu, order before 3PM

💡 What Affects the Final Price

The per-bottle and monthly rental prices are listed in the table above. What you pay in total typically depends on:

  • the number of bottles in the order
  • whether it is a single order or a recurring schedule, since the per-bottle rate differs
  • whether you add a pump or cooler rental, which is charged monthly
  • the size of a bulk order for a guesthouse, office or event; for anything unusual it helps to confirm the total with us before delivery

🔍 When You Need Something Other Than Delivery

Bottle delivery is simple, but sometimes the real problem is not the water supply:

  • if your own pump or cooler has stopped working, renting one is the quick fix; a fault in the wiring or a cooler that trips the power needs an electrician
  • if pipes leak, a tank misbehaves or water pressure is poor, that is a job for a plumber
  • a site visit is not normally needed for bottles: your address, the number of bottles and a few words about access are usually enough

Can I set up a recurring delivery?

Yes, weekly or bi-weekly delivery can be scheduled so you never run out, with a small discount for regular customers.

Do you rent water pumps or coolers?

Yes, both are available for rent if yours is broken or missing.

How do I contact BroService?

Call +66 80-870-5704, WhatsApp, Telegram or email. Available 8:00–20:00 daily.

How many 19L bottles will my household need?

It depends on how many people live there and how much you use for drinking and cooking. Tell us the household size, start with a modest weekly or bi-weekly amount and adjust after the first deliveries.

Can you supply a guesthouse, office or event?
